6Rating regression (ridge)
one fit per role; whole matches are the CV unit
Batting: $v=\mathrm{ctx}+B_i+P\,C_i-(W_j+P\,K_j)+\varepsilon$
Bowling: $v=\mathrm{ctx}+W_j+P\,K_j-(B_i+P\,C_i)+\varepsilon$
$i$ = batter, $j$ = bowler; ctx = fixed effects (innings × phase × $P$, competition group)
$\min\ \sum(v-\hat v)^2+\lambda_B\sum(B^2+W^2)$
$\qquad+\ \lambda_C\sum(C^2+K^2)+0.001\lVert\mathrm{ctx}\rVert^2$
$\lambda_B=300$, $\ \lambda_C=3000$

$\textcolor{#ff5c9a}{R_i=B_i+C_i}$
