## The two 'wow' insights (taken straight from the results)

- **Bowlers:** 8 of the 10 listed bowlers are spinners (20 of the 55 in the eligible, active pool are), source `outputs/step3_summary.json`. Honest context: spinners bowl 35.0% of pressure balls vs 37.2% of live balls, so this is not because spinners face more pressure; role/captaincy selection is not controlled.
- **Batters:** Shreyas Iyer's strike rate rises from 148.2 on ordinary balls to 172.8 on pressure balls (`outputs/evidence_cards.md`), the largest uplift among the 10 listed batters. Honest context: 298 pressure balls in 20 matches; the reliability work says a separable clutch trait is not measurable (shift split-half 0.13), so present it as 'he scored fast when it mattered', not 'he is clutch'.
