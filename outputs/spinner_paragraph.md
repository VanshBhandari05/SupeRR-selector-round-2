Eight of the ten listed bowlers are spinners (Sunil Narine, Kuldeep Yadav, Rashid Khan, Varun Chakravarthy, Wanindu Hasaranga, Axar Patel, Noor Ahmad, Krunal Pandya); the two pace bowlers are Josh Hazlewood and Jasprit Bumrah. Exposure does not explain this: spinners bowl 35.0% of pressure balls against 37.2% of all live balls in the rating sample (a ratio of 0.94), so they are not handed more pressure overs than their share of play. Spinners are 20 of the 55 bowlers in the eligible, active pool (36%) but 8 of the 10 on the list. The list therefore over-represents spinners relative to the pool, and because spinners are not over-exposed to pressure, the gap comes from the value they add per pressure ball, not from volume. The data do not say why (pitch conditions, match-ups and captains' choices are not modelled), so this is reported as an observation, not an explanation.
