EVERY PLACE THE RR STAGE ABSTAINED (and why)

A. Facts that need data outside the supplied files: "not in supplied data"
 1. IPL 2027 purse / remaining budget: not in supplied data.
 2. IPL 2027 retention rules and which RR players are retained or released: not in supplied data.
 3. Squad lists after the IPL 2026 listing in competition_team_player_mapping.csv: not in supplied data.
 4. Contract values, contract length, buy-out or trade status of any player: not in supplied data.
 5. Player availability (injury, national duty, auction pool entry): not in supplied data.
 6. Whether a 2026-listed RR player is "contracted" for 2027: not in supplied data. The 2026 RR squad listing (27 players) is used only as a proxy for "on RR's books in the data".
 7. How many overseas players RR will hold in 2027: not in supplied data. Only the derived-nationality count of the 2026 listing is reported (9 overseas identified, 8 unknown).
 8. Auction price or market value of any candidate: not in supplied data.

B. Nationality (Indian / overseas) is not a field in the data
 9. Nationality is derived from T20I squad listings (a player listed only for India = Indian; any other single national team = overseas); an A-team-only listing is used as a fallback.
10. Players with no T20I or A-team listing get "not in supplied data": Sunil Narine (bowler #1) and Krunal Pandya (bowler #10) among the 40 candidates; 8 of the 27 RR 2026-squad players
    (Sandeep Sharma, Sushant Mishra, Kuldeep Sen, Shubham Dubey, Yash Punja, Vignesh Puthur, Ravi Singh, Brijesh Sharma). They are not guessed.
11. Nationality is not checked for eligibility under the overseas rule (e.g. associate-nation or other-status players): not in supplied data.

C. Cells suppressed for sample size (under 40 pressure balls in the slot)
12. Batting 7+: 36 of the 40 listed players are suppressed (14 have zero balls there). All 20 top batters are suppressed, so no listed batter can be offered for this slot.
13. Death pace: of 30 listed players with any bowling rows, 22 are suppressed (20 have zero pace-death balls: spinners or no bowling).
14. Middle pace: 21 of 30 suppressed (19 with zero).
15. 10 of the 40 listed players have no bowling rows in the rating sample at all, so they are absent from the two bowling slots.
16. Squad evidence: any RR 2026-squad player under 40 slot balls is listed as "suppressed" in rr_02b_rr_squad_slot_evidence.csv, and cannot count as covering a gap.
17. Candidate rows for the other seven (non-target) slots are in rr_03_candidate_slot_evidence.csv with the same suppression rule (247 of 340 slot cells suppressed overall).

D. Gaps reported as not distinguishable from par (not ranked as findings)
18. Death pace, middle pace, batting 3-4, powerplay spin, and all five slots at or above par: not distinguishable from par at 80% or 95%.
19. Batting 7+ is distinguishable at 80% only, not at 95%; reported as weak support, one of ten slots.
20. In sensitivity, death-pace gap is never distinguishable in any variant; middle pace only under the top 30% variant; powerplay pace only under top 10% and within-innings.
21. RR's overall batting and overall bowling pressure gaps are not distinguishable from par.

E. Things the data cannot separate
22. Venue effects: RR gaps are not adjusted for RR's home grounds beyond the leverage model's scoring-environment tier. Not controlled in this stage by design (no retuning).
23. Selection bias: the candidates are the top 20 on the same data; their slot excess is unshrunk and biased upward. Not corrected (no new model was fitted for it).
24. Bowler type: pace/spin comes from the supplied pace_or_spin field at ball level; no bowler style detail (e.g. wrist vs finger spin) was used.
25. Slot-level coverage uses a player's career record across IPL and T20I, not RR-only; RR-only slot records were not separable at usable sample sizes.
26. Pressure flag variants: only those in the existing grid (10/15/20/25/30% pooled, within-innings 20%). The win-probability-without-recalibration variant was never run in Stage 6 and is not run here either.

F. Method choices flagged for review (not abstentions)
27. A draft check in stage9d ("mean adjusted value near 0") was wrong (adjusted value is not centred by construction). It was replaced by the correct check (dWP0 centred within each Stage-4 bin); the replacement and the reason are recorded in src/stage9d_crosscheck.py.
28. Top-10% threshold is in the existing grid, so it is run and shown, but the "survives in k of 4" counts that match your brief use 15%, 25%, 30% and within-innings only; the 5-variant counts include top 10%.
